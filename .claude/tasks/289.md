# Task 289 — Sweep finished units' worktrees in /task-clean and /runbook-clean

Target: claude

## Goal
Give the two pruners the sweep the `worktree-isolation` feature assigns
them, so a worktree left behind by a landed, failed-then-finished or
interrupted unit is removed once its unit is terminal. `/task-clean` already
sweeps `park/task-*` branches beside its prune; it gains the matching sweep
of `wt/task-<N>` worktrees and branches whose task is `[DONE]` or `[SKIP]`,
planned and reported in the same place. `/runbook-clean` gains the sweep of
`wt/runbook-<id>-step-<n>` worktrees and branches whose step is `[x]`, or
whose runbook the run is archiving. A worktree whose unit is not finished is
never swept: it may be the only copy of the work.

## Acceptance criteria
- `/task-clean` lists, in its plan, every `wt/task-<N>` worktree and branch
  (from `git worktree list --porcelain` and `git branch --list 'wt/task-*'`)
  whose task is `[DONE]` or `[SKIP]` in `TASKS.md`, or whose task has no
  summary block (archived and terminal per `resolution.md` § *The archive*),
  beside its `park/task-*` sweep, and removes each on confirmation —
  worktree removed, then branch deleted. A worktree whose task carries any
  other status, `[IN PROGRESS]` and `[PARKED]` included, is never listed.
- `/runbook-clean` lists, in its plan, every `wt/runbook-<id>-step-<n>`
  worktree and branch whose step is `[x]` in its runbook's body, or whose
  runbook is among those the run archives, and removes each on confirmation
  under the same rule; a step with any other marker keeps its worktree.
- Both sweeps are local: no remote listing and no remote delete, since
  `wt/<unit>` worktrees and branches are never pushed; nothing is skipped
  under `--no-push`, and the sweep runs under `--no-commit` as the park
  sweep does — it is the prune's effect, not a commit step.
- A remove or delete that fails is reported as a *Follow-ups* item naming
  the path and the command, never a halt and never retried; a run that only
  sweeps commits nothing.
- The sweep cites `../task-engine/references/worktree.md` (from
  `skills/task-clean/`) and `../skills/task-engine/references/worktree.md`
  (from `commands/runbook-clean.md`) as the rule's home and states only its
  own deviations; `./scripts/check-home-paths.sh` passes.
- Both features' `version:` are bumped; root `VERSION` is bumped (patch)
  with a matching `CHANGELOG.md` section; `./scripts/check-changelog.sh` and
  `./scripts/check-routing.sh` pass.

## Decisions
- Sweeping is the pruners' job, not the orchestrators': a leftover worktree
  is the record of a failed or interrupted unit until someone decides it is
  finished — the reasoning that put the `park/task-*` sweep in
  `/task-clean`, applied again.
- `/runbook-clean` reads `worktree.md` through `/runbook-run`'s new
  `requires: skill:task-engine` (task 288); it declares no requirement of
  its own, since `requires:` is one level deep and the command already
  requires the orchestrator.
