# Task 222 — Rename the `stop hook refused` literal to `stop hook ignored on runbook WIP`

Target: claude

## Goal
When a cloud sandbox's Stop hook blocks a turn on the dirty tree an in-flight
step creates by design, `/runbook-run` replies with the fixed literal
`stop hook refused` and ends the turn. The behaviour is right and costs one
forced turn; the wording is not. "Refused" reads as a failure of the run and
says nothing about why the block was safe to ignore. Rename the literal to
`stop hook ignored on runbook WIP`, which names both the action and its reason.

## Acceptance criteria
- The literal reads `stop hook ignored on runbook WIP` at all three sites: the
  reply block in `skills/runbook-run/SKILL.md`, the expectation in
  `docs/reference.md`, and the entry in `.claude/context/features.md`.
- The condition is unchanged: it applies only when the sole uncommitted changes
  are the runbook body and the index the run itself just wrote.
- The fall-through is unchanged — anything else dirty is handled normally, which
  is the property that keeps a genuinely forgotten commit from being swallowed.
- No tool call, no `git status` and no explanation still accompany the reply; the
  one-forced-turn cost is unchanged.
- Historical `CHANGELOG.md` entries naming the old literal are **not** rewritten.
- Root `VERSION` and `CHANGELOG.md` bumped per CLAUDE.md § Versioning (patch);
  `./scripts/check-changelog.sh` exits clean.

## Decisions
- **Wording only.** No part of the condition, the fall-through or the cost model
  changes, so nothing downstream of the literal needs to move.
- **History is left alone.** A CHANGELOG entry records what shipped at the time;
  rewriting it to match today's wording would make the record false.
