# Task 286 — Adopt the interaction policy in the remaining interactive features

Target: claude

## Goal
Make `/context-build`, `/context-update`, `/context-convert`,
`/refactor-codebase`, `/refactor-tests`, `/doc-consolidate`,
`/project-setup`, `/session-save` and `/follow-ups-resolve` adopt feature
`interaction-policy`.

## Acceptance criteria
- Each declares `requires: skill:interaction-engine`, cites it relatively,
  accepts both flags.
- Class 1: the STOPs of the three context skills; the refactor plan gates
  except HIGH-risk items (class 2); `/project-setup`'s final "Approve and
  run?"; `/session-save`'s pointer prompt. Class 2: `/doc-consolidate`'s
  judgement gate, `/follow-ups-resolve`'s list approval, every other gate.
- The `-y` flags of `/context-update` and `/context-convert` keep their
  meaning. `/unity-mcp-setup` is not touched.
- Gate summaries and closing reports follow the output rules; questions the
  question rules. No restated engine rule remains. Feature `version:`s and
  root `VERSION` bumped with a `CHANGELOG.md` section;
  `check-changelog.sh`, `check-home-paths.sh` pass.

## Decisions
- `/project-setup` gets the dependency and flags only; offering the
  `Interaction policy` line in its wizard is setup-sync's, not this task's.
