# Task 299 — Update documentation for feature `parallel-runbooks`

Target: claude

## Goal
Feature `parallel-runbooks` ships the `Parallelism:` header line,
`/runbook-run --parallel <N|max>` with graph scheduling and step worktrees,
the authoring duty in `/runbook-create` and the lines gained by
`/runbook-describe` and `/pipeline-check` (tasks 295–298). The documentation
describing the runbook schema, `/runbook-run`'s loop, contracts and cadence,
`/runbook-create`, the readers and the lint catalogue is now out of date and
must reflect the shipped behaviour, and no longer state that steps are
sequential always.

## Acceptance criteria
- `README.md`, `docs/reference.md` and, where it lists skill flags,
  `docs/cli-help.txt` describe the header line, the flag and its two forms,
  the three refusals, the graph as the schedule, the barriers and the
  resume signal, in the register each already uses, without restating the
  skill bodies.
- `.claude/context/runbook-suite.md`, `runbook-run.md`,
  `runbook-run-loop.md` and `runbook-run-contracts.md` describe selection,
  the spawned prompt's fourth placeholder, the landing, the cadence and the
  one-run rule as they now stand; `runbook-create.md`, `runbook-readers.md`
  and `pipeline-readers.md` describe the authoring duty, the describe lines
  and the probe; `features.md` and `INDEX.md` are consistent with them, and
  `INDEX.md`'s `Last updated` is advanced.
- Root `VERSION` is not bumped and `CHANGELOG.md` gains no entry: the change
  is confined to documentation, which `CLAUDE.md` § Versioning exempts.
