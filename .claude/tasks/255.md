# Task 255 — Give `/runbook-run`'s closing report the two groups, and number every closing report's Needs you items

Target: claude

## Goal
`/runbook-run`'s closing report is one entry per step plus a feature
completion block; `/task-implement`'s and `/task-review`'s close in two
groups, Needs you then For the record. Bring the runbook report into that
shape, keeping its per-step entries, and number the Needs you items in
every closing report so the user answers by number, the way `/follow-ups`'
numbering is the handle.

## Acceptance criteria
- `skills/runbook-run/SKILL.md`'s closing report — at completion, at a
  `--to` / `--only` / `--steps` bound and at a failure halt alike — has two
  groups in this order. **Needs you**: numbered items, each awaiting a
  decision, any length: the feature completion candidates with the flip
  question, a failed step with its reason, a `[~]` step to resume, the
  steps left outside the range. **For the record**: one line per step, in
  the shape `<step n> — <outcome, commit sha and diffstat> — <what changed
  in one line; decision or wrong premise flagged; questions relayed and
  their answers>`, followed by any run-level deviation on one line. An
  empty group prints its heading and `none`. CHAT OUTPUT's "quiet between
  steps" rule and the `/follow-ups` call after the report are unchanged.
- `skills/task-implement/SKILL.md` THE CLOSING REPORT: the Needs you items
  are numbered (`1.`, `2.`, …), and the section says the number is the
  handle the user replies with; the For the record shape is unchanged.
  `./delegated-runs.md`'s sixth field and the parent's rendering of it are
  consistent with that numbering.
- `skills/task-review/SKILL.md` THE REPORT: the closing section's Needs you
  items are numbered the same way.
- `.claude/domain/features/runbook-suite.md` states the agreed design change
  — the closing report's two groups and numbered Needs you items — in every
  passage that stated the old shape; `.claude/domain/features/task-peer-review.md`
  states the numbered Needs you items in every passage describing the two
  groups. Nothing else in either document changes.
- `docs/reference.md` (§ `/runbook-run`, § `/task-implement`, § `/task-review`),
  `.claude/domain/task-workflow.md` and `.claude/context/features.md`
  describe the shape and the numbering.
- `skills/runbook-run` `version:` minor bump; `skills/task-implement` and
  `skills/task-review` patch bumps; root `VERSION` minor bump; `CHANGELOG.md`
  entry; `./scripts/check-changelog.sh`, `./scripts/check-routing.sh` and
  `./scripts/check-home-paths.sh` silent.

## Decisions
- **Design change agreed (user decision, 2026-09-21):** `/runbook-run`'s
  closing report takes the Needs you / For the record groups, per-step
  entries kept under For the record; Needs you items are numbered in every
  closing report. `.claude/domain/features/runbook-suite.md` and
  `.claude/domain/features/task-peer-review.md` are normally `/architect`'s;
  this task updates them for exactly this and for nothing else. A further
  design decision met at implementation time is not covered: leave that
  passage untouched and name it under Needs you as a precise follow-up.
- Numbering starts at 1 per report and carries no meaning beyond the reply
  handle; a report with one item still numbers it.
