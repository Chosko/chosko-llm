# Task 282 — Adopt the interaction policy in the `task-*` suite

Target: claude

## Goal
Make `/task-implement`, `/task-add`, `/task-setup`, `/task-iterate`,
`/task-clean` and the `task-engine` amend arm adopt feature
`interaction-policy`: declare `requires: skill:interaction-engine`, cite its
files, accept `--attended` / `--unattended`, tag every gate with its class,
and delete every passage the engine now states.

## Acceptance criteria
- Each listed feature's frontmatter `requires:` gains
  `skill:interaction-engine`, its supporting-files table gains the engine
  rows, and it accepts both flags (both together is the engine's argument
  error). Citations are relative per the home-path guard.
- `/task-implement`: UNATTENDED resolves via `policy.md` (flag, handed-down
  policy, CLAUDE.md line, attended); its own resolution passage is replaced
  by the citation; `--attended` added; `--skip-parked` keeps its refusals;
  the refusals of `--unattended` beside `--no-commit` / on a non-git VCS
  apply to the typed flag only. The feature `[DONE]`-flip proposal and the
  skip-tests "Proceed?" are tagged class 1; the feature-completion proposal
  is rewritten to the question rules. `delegated-runs.md`'s "verbatim and
  unabridged" draft relay becomes the plain-summary rule with `show`, and its
  resolved-flag list carries the resolved policy under both values.
- `task-engine`: `parking.md`'s parked-question lines and `stale.md`'s
  stale-task prompt rewritten to the question rules; `parking.md`'s
  "prompts take their default" passage reconciled with class 1 (the
  feature-flip proposal passes, flipping); `amend.md`'s single gate tagged
  class 1 and its "Amended …" line rewritten plain-language-first; the
  map in `SKILL.md` updated where its rows describe what changed.
- `/task-add`: "Approve and write?" tagged class 1; the manual-interventions
  block shown as a summary like the rest of the digest; the
  design-change and orphan questions follow the question rules; a diverging
  design-change point under `unattended` is a real decision and stops the
  run (task-add cannot park).
- `/task-setup`'s overwrite and `/task-clean`'s prune and backfill gates
  tagged class 3; `/task-iterate`'s gates tagged (class 2 unless the feature
  doc lists them class 1); their closing reports follow the output rules.
- No restated engine rule remains in these bodies. Each changed feature's
  `version:` bumped; root `VERSION` minor-bumped with a `CHANGELOG.md`
  section; `check-changelog.sh` and `check-home-paths.sh` pass.

## Decisions
- `/task-review` takes no dependency: it reports findings and has no gate or
  question (feature doc § Adoption does not list it).
- The parked-question template is rewritten once, in `task-engine`; the
  runbook side of parking is task 283's.
