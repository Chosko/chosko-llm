# Task 168 — Reconcile the runbook suite's documentation with IDs, describe and the spawn relay

Target: claude

## Goal
Tasks 165–167 change three things the runbook suite's documentation states as
settled: that runbooks have no numeric id, that the suite has five artifacts,
and that a step needing a nested spawn is past verified ground. Bring
`.claude/domain/features/runbook-suite.md` and README's runbook section into
line with what shipped.

## Acceptance criteria
- README's `runbook-*` section lists `/runbook-describe`, shows the ID as an
  accepted argument form, and reflects `/runbook-list`'s new columns.
- `.claude/domain/features/runbook-suite.md` is reconciled with what shipped on
  exactly these 6 points — editing it is authorised for this task, see
  Decisions:
  1. § *The store* and § *The index block*: "there is no counter and no numeric
     id" is replaced by the counter, the ID, and the rule that the name stays
     canonical and remains the filename.
  2. § *The five shipped artifacts*, the ship-order paragraph, and the
     `requires:` graph: six artifacts, with `/runbook-describe` and its
     `requires: skill:runbook-run` edge.
  3. § *`/runbook-list` — the read side*: the rendered-shape sample gains the ID
     and title columns.
  4. § *The body schema* / § *A step*: the optional `Needs:` field, its three
     values, that `/runbook-create` authors it and `/runbook-describe` prints
     it, and that inference is a labelled fallback.
  5. § *The depth budget* and the *Hard contracts* list: the depth-4 warning is
     superseded by the relay; "steps are sequential, always" gains the stated
     suspended-caller exception; the three result cases become four.
  6. § *Scope and non-goals*, "the orchestrator doing any of the work itself":
     it now spawns a child on a caller's behalf and reads neither the request
     nor the result file.
  Nothing else in the document changes.
- No `VERSION` bump and no `CHANGELOG.md` entry. This task is confined to
  `README.md` and `.claude/domain/`, which CLAUDE.md § Versioning exempts —
  nothing a user receives behaves differently because of it.

## Decisions
- **Editing `.claude/domain/features/runbook-suite.md` is explicitly authorised
  for this task (user decision, 2026-09-02), for the 6 reconciliations listed
  above and for nothing else. Do not ask for permission at implementation time —
  the permission is already granted.** That document is normally `/architect`'s,
  and anything beyond those points still belongs to `/architect`. These are
  factual reconciliations with shipped behaviour, not redesigns; if one turns
  out to need a design decision rather than a wording fix, stop and say so
  instead of deciding it here.
- **The documentation pass is one task, not three.** Three tasks each editing
  the same `/architect`-owned document would leave it in partial states and
  invite conflicts; one task after all three land reconciles it once against
  what actually shipped.
- **This task deliberately bumps nothing.** It is the converse half of
  CLAUDE.md's rule — a change that does not bump `VERSION` gets no `CHANGELOG`
  entry.
