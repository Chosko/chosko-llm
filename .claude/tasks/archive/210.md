## Goal
Feature `runbook-inline` (.claude/domain/features/runbook-inline.md) adds
`/runbook-run --inline`. The documentation for the runbook area still says,
without qualification, that `/runbook-run` starts one fresh subagent per step
and never does a step's work itself. Bring it in line with what task 209
shipped: those are default-mode contracts, and `--inline` is the one opt-in
exception.
