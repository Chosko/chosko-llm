## Goal
A spawned `/runbook-run` produces noisy mid-step chat: every forced turn (a
Stop hook firing on the in-flight step's dirty tree, a relay notification)
gets a free-form explanation — "The other uncommitted files are step 82's
implementation. It is paused while its review child runs, and /task-implement
commits it after the review…", "Step 83 is still in flight: its work and the
runbook's in-progress markers get committed when it finishes." Replace that
with two fixed forms. At step start the run prints one progress line,
`Step <n> running. Current run progress (<k>/<m>). Total runbook progress (<x>/<y>).`,
which is also the only status line a forced mid-step turn may print besides
the spawn relay's own lines. And the Stop-hook reply gains a second case:
while a spawned step is in flight, whatever is dirty is answered with exactly
`stop hook ignored on subagent WIP` — no tool call, no explanation. The
relay's `Waiting on the relayed child for step <n>.` and the step-end
`Step <n> done (<sha>). Starting step <n+1>.` stay exactly as they are.

## Acceptance criteria
- `skills/runbook-run/SKILL.md` § CHAT OUTPUT states the progress line, its
  exact shape, when it is printed (once per step, when the step is spawned or
  its execution phase begins, the run's first step included), and that on a
  forced mid-step turn it is the only status line allowed besides the spawn
  relay's own lines; the step-end line and the never-suppressed items stay as
  they are.
- The counters are defined in that section: `k` is this step's position among
  the steps this run executes, from 1; `m` is the run's planned size, fixed at
  launch — the smaller of `--steps N` (when given) and the number of
  selectable `[ ]` steps in range — and not recomputed when a step parks or
  fails; `x` is the number of `[x]` steps in the whole runbook plus the step
  now starting; `y` is every step in the runbook.
- § COMMIT CADENCE's *The Stop-hook reply* covers two cases in one section,
  rewritten as a whole (Editing Discipline rule 7): between steps, only the
  runbook and the index dirty → `stop hook ignored on runbook WIP` (unchanged);
  while a spawned step is in flight (spawned, result not yet arrived) →
  `stop hook ignored on subagent WIP`, whatever is dirty, since nothing but the
  step's subagent and the two bookkeeping files can have written the tree
  then. Both replies: no tool call, no `git status`, end the turn. The
  fall-through for any other state stays.
- The subagent-WIP case applies in spawned mode only; the section says so, and
  `--inline` keeps the runbook-WIP rule alone.
- `.claude/domain/features/runbook-suite.md` states the agreed design change —
  the chat contract gains the step-start progress line, which is also the only
  status line on a forced mid-step turn — in every passage that stated the old
  one-line-per-step-end contract. Nothing else in the document changes.
- `docs/reference.md`'s `/runbook-run` entry reflects the progress line if it
  describes the run's chat output.
- `skills/runbook-run/SKILL.md` frontmatter `version:` bumped (patch); root
  `VERSION` bumped (patch) with a matching `CHANGELOG.md` section;
  `./scripts/check-changelog.sh` and `./scripts/check-home-paths.sh` silent.

## Decisions
- **Counter semantics (user decision, 2026-09-30):** `m` is fixed at launch
  so the ratio reads as "how far through what this run set out to do"; a
  parked or failed step does not shrink it.
- **Subagent-WIP reply is spawned-mode only (user decision, 2026-09-30):**
  under `--inline` there is no subagent, and the session's own dirty files are
  its own work to track.
- **Design change agreed (user decision, 2026-09-30):**
  `.claude/domain/features/runbook-suite.md` § The chat contract: "one line
  per step at that step's end … narrates nothing else" → a step-start progress
  line plus the step-end line, the progress line being the only status on a
  forced mid-step turn. `.claude/domain/features/runbook-suite.md` is normally
  `/architect`'s; this task updates it for exactly this and for nothing else.
  A further design decision met at implementation time is not covered: leave
  that passage untouched and name it under *Follow-ups* as a precise
  follow-up.
