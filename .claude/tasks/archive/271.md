## Goal
Extend `follow-ups-resolve` (task 270) with how it behaves beside a running
`/runbook-run`, and wire `/runbook-run` to it. The user may resolve and
approve follow-ups mid-run under the full protocol, but **nothing executes
until the run ends**, even with the whole list approved. A follow-up that
arises mid-run is printed once, as a single line, when it arises; the list
is never re-printed on its own — it is on demand: `/follow-ups` invoked
mid-run prints what was collected so far, Approved first, then Awaiting
approval. At the run's end the closing report's Follow-ups group carries the
items still awaiting approval, and the approved ones start executing without
waiting — unless an unapproved item is a precondition for an approved one.
Whenever the user approves items, they move into the approved list, merged
with existing items where convenient.

## Decisions
- **List on demand, never auto-rewritten (user decision, 2026-09-30):** an
  arising follow-up is one line; the full list prints only when the user asks
  (`/follow-ups`) and at the closing report.
- **Unapproved items at the run's end appear once, in the closing report's
  Follow-ups group** — not as a separate list after it.
- **Executing after the report is conversation after the run**, so COMMIT
  CADENCE and the report's no-commit contract are untouched.
- **Design change agreed (user decision, 2026-09-30):**
  `.claude/domain/features/runbook-suite.md` § The chat contract: "narrates
  nothing else" → gains the arising-follow-up line as a third never-suppressed
  item alongside relayed questions and relay lines.
  `.claude/domain/features/runbook-suite.md` is normally `/architect`'s; this
  task updates it for exactly this and for nothing else. A further design
  decision met at implementation time is not covered: leave that passage
  untouched and name it under *Follow-ups* as a precise follow-up.
