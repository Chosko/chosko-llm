# Task 294 — Update documentation for feature `parallel-task-runs`

Target: claude

## Goal
Feature `parallel-task-runs` ships `/task-implement --parallel <N|max>`,
its scheduler, barriers and landing, the `Files:` overlap rule and the
`Parallel-safe files for /task-implement:` marker (tasks 292–293). The
documentation describing `/task-implement`'s delegated runs, the task
engine's eligibility clause and the project's `CLAUDE.md` markers is now
out of date and must reflect the shipped behaviour.

## Acceptance criteria
- `README.md`, `docs/reference.md` and, where it lists skill flags,
  `docs/cli-help.txt` describe the flag, its two forms, its refusals, the
  overlap rule and the marker in the register each already uses, without
  restating `delegated-runs.md`.
- `.claude/context/task-implement.md` and
  `.claude/context/task-implement-delegation.md` describe the parallel
  scheduler, the barriers and the landing, and no longer state that agents
  are spawned one at a time, never parallel; `.claude/context/task-engine.md`
  names the in-flight clause; `.claude/context/features.md` and
  `.claude/context/INDEX.md` are consistent with them, and `INDEX.md`'s
  `Last updated` is advanced.
- `.claude/domain/task-workflow.md` § Delegated runs states the current
  rule — sequential by default, concurrent under `--parallel` with the
  worktree protocol — in place of "Agents sequential, never parallel", and
  its `/task-implement` discipline names the marker beside the testing
  policy.
- Root `VERSION` is not bumped and `CHANGELOG.md` gains no entry: the change
  is confined to documentation, which `CLAUDE.md` § Versioning exempts.
